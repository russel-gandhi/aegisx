<objective>
Implement all 10 deterministic compliance rules from Bible Section 3.3 as a Rego bundle that OPA actually serves, with an `opa test` suite meeting the Critical-review bar: unit, negative, edge-case, and integration coverage, independent of the application.

This plan covers Build-Map ticket **SENT-1-03** — the only `Critical`-review ticket in Stage 1 (BRANCHING.md §4 and §6). It is not a smoke-test ticket. Per CLAUDE.md Rule 6, "the rule exists" is not done; "the rule fires on the record it was written to catch, and demonstrably does not fire on the record it was not" is done.

This is also the phase's deterministic-first keystone. Every one of these 10 checks is a compliance threshold, which Bible Section 1.3 forbids any LLM from ever evaluating. The rules are the mechanism that makes that constraint real rather than aspirational.

Purpose: C1's confidence score in Phase 3 is only meaningful because an independent policy engine, not a model, decided whether each record violates a regulation.

<critical_findings>
Two defects in the Bible's literal Section 3.3 text were identified during planning. Both are load-bearing: transcribing Section 3.3 verbatim produces a bundle that either fails to parse or contains six rules that can never fire. Both corrections preserve every rule ID, every severity, every regulatory citation, and every threshold number exactly. Neither is a licence to redesign anything else.

**Finding A — Rego v0 syntax on a v1-default engine.** The Bible writes partial-set rules as `violation[{...}] { ... }`. `docker-compose.yml` runs `openpolicyagent/opa:1.19.1-debug`, and OPA 1.0+ parses Rego v1 by default, which requires the `contains` and `if` keywords. Verbatim transcription fails `opa test` and `opa check`. Source: 02-RESEARCH.md lines 445-468, verified against OPA's own v0-upgrade guide.

**Finding B — `time.diff(...)[2]` is a calendar day-component, not elapsed days.** `time.diff` returns `[years, months, days, hours, minutes, seconds]`, so index `[2]` is the day remainder within a month, never a total. `[2] > 365` can never be true, and `[2] > 30` is true at most on the single boundary day. Applied literally, rules 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, and 8 never fire — including against the seeded records in plan 02-01 that exist specifically to trigger them. `time.diff` also normalises to a non-negative difference, so the `> 0` form in rules 6 and 7 would additionally fire on records that are not yet due. POL-01 requires every rule to pass a *positive* fixture, which is impossible under the literal text; the correction is therefore forced by the requirement itself, not chosen.

Both are recorded in `policies/BIBLE-DEVIATIONS.md` (Task 3) and flagged for the SENT-7-05 bible-reconciliation review, per CLAUDE.md's "drift is reconciled explicitly" rule.
</critical_findings>
