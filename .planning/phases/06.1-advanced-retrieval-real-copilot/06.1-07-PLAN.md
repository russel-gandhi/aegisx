<objective>
Close HARD-04: give this phase's retrieval pipeline a measured quality number instead
of an impression. A small labelled corpus, a labelled query set, standard IR metrics
(precision@k, recall@k, MRR), and a runner that reports dense-only, lexical-only and
full fused+reranked configurations side by side.

Purpose: `06.1-RESEARCH.md` flags two constants — `RRF_K = 60` and
`RERANK_RELEVANCE_THRESHOLD = 0.35` — as conventional defaults never validated against
this project's own corpus, and rates the reranker's score calibration unvalidated. A
retrieval system whose quality nobody measured is a retrieval system nobody can defend
in a compliance conversation, which is precisely the conversation this product exists
to win.
Output: `app/retrieval/evaluation.py`, a labelled fixture set, and a baseline-regression
test.

Scope boundary: this plan measures and records. It does not retune the pipeline. If the
measurement shows a constant is badly chosen, that is a finding for the phase summary and
a candidate follow-up, not a silent in-plan change to a value another plan set.
</objective>
