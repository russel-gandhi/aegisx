<objective>
Fill the ranking and provenance depth behind the `hybrid_retrieve()` seam plan 06.1-02
established: lexical BM25 candidates fused with the dense candidates by Reciprocal Rank
Fusion (RAG-03), one batched cross-encoder-style reranking pass (RAG-04), the
deterministic relevance gate moved onto the reranker score, and the Section 15.7
provenance completed with parent-context and graph-derived evidence (RAG-05, AGT-01).

Purpose: dense-only retrieval misses exact identifier matches — a GxP corpus is full
of `URS-042`, `ANNEX11-S4-DOC-001`, `TC-2026-042` tokens that lexical search finds
reliably and embeddings blur. Bible Section 15.2/15.3 specifies the fusion + rerank
pipeline; this plan implements it for real.
Output: `app/retrieval/lexical.py`, a complete `app/retrieval/hybrid_search.py`, and
one new `use_for` entry in the existing provider router.

Boundary restated (D-08): scoring which chunks are relevant is retrieval, not a
compliance decision — it may use models. Deciding whether evidence is *sufficient*
is a deterministic numeric comparison in Python, and C1's PASS/FAIL grading stays
exactly where it is, untouched by this plan.
</objective>
