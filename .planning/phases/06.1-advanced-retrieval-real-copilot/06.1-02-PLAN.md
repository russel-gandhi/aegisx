<objective>
Give the fully-built-but-never-called compiled `StateGraph` its first live HTTP caller,
and give A1 a real retrieval body (D-05, D-06, RAG-06, AGT-01).

A user asks a free-text question; the route builds a real `AgentState` and awaits
`compiled_graph.ainvoke(...)`; A1 embeds the query, runs a real Qdrant vector search
scoped to the system, applies a deterministic relevance-threshold gate, and returns
ranked evidence with Bible Section 15.7 provenance; the route then synthesizes a
grounded, citing answer through the dormant `task="synthesis"` provider slot and
returns it with its evidence and stage trace.

Purpose: `06.1-RESEARCH.md`'s single most load-bearing finding is that the graph as
built today produces no answer — `final_synthesis` is C3's action-routing sentence
("Execution complete. 0 action(s) queued for approval, 0 blocked.") and A7 is gated
off unless remediation was requested. A route that returned `final_synthesis` verbatim
would ship a confidently misleading non-answer. The synthesis step therefore lives in
the route, after `ainvoke()` returns, leaving the fixed topology untouched.
Output: `POST /api/copilot/investigate`, `app/retrieval/hybrid_search.py`, a real `run_a1`.

Ranking depth (BM25 + Reciprocal Rank Fusion + batched cross-encoder reranking) and
provenance depth (parent-context and graph expansion) land in plan 06.1-03 behind the
same `hybrid_retrieve()` seam this plan establishes. That is a fill, not a rearchitecture.
</objective>
