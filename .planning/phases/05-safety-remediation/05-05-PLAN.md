<objective>
Make the approval loop visible and operable in the browser: a persistent role selector supplies identity on every write, a Generate CAPA button on a verified finding creates a proposal, that proposal is pushed live over the existing copilot WebSocket, and the Action / Approval Centre renders it from server-trusted data with working Approve and Reject controls.

**User story:** **As a** QA/IT operator watching a GxP system, **I want to** see a proposed remediation appear in my approval queue the moment it is generated and act on it there, **so that** nothing is executed behind my back and my decision is the one recorded.

Purpose: this is the half of Phase 5 that a human can see. REM-04 and UI-02 are only provable through a real browser-facing loop, and REM-03's "approval dialog renders only from server-trusted metadata" is a claim about this UI specifically.
Output: the WebSocket broadcast extension, the frontend identity layer, and the live Approval Centre.
</objective>

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| server WebSocket -> every connected browser | Proposal contents fan out to all clients regardless of session |
