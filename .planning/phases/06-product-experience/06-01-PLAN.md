| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-06-04 | Spoofing | POST /api/copilot/query carries no identity/session binding | low | accept | Makes no write, no RBAC-relevant decision, reads no per-role data — an unauthenticated caller gains nothing beyond what any browser tab can already do by importing `detect_injection` conceptually (it is a pure function, not a secret).
